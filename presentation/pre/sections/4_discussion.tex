  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Clang SA\sref{clangsa} beats every local LM}: \res{df-clang_sa}{macro} at \res{df-clang_sa}{lat}, no GPU. Path-sensitive analysis still matters

    \item \textbf{One local device}: one RTX 3090; other hardware will shift latency

\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\noindent\textbf{Construct validity.}
Juliet is synthetic: its leaks are small, local and labeled by construction, so our scores do not transfer to real code.
Our leak definition counts memory still held by a global at exit and leaks on \code{realloc} failure.
A tool that follows the narrower ``unreachable at exit'' notion (e.g.\ plain LSan) is penalized on these 176 bad units by design, which is why dynamic tools are a reference and not ranked. % src: data/build/verify_report.md (68 global + 108 realloc_fail)
\Dead{} tests one specific skill, ignoring frees behind always-false guards over file-local constants.
A detector that resolves such guards is not necessarily path-sensitive in general, and one that fails may still handle other infeasible paths.
The guards were designed against our own probes; a learner could still find another residual cue, although the BoW AUROC is \res{x}{probe_bow_auroc_layer3} on \Dead{} and stems from intrinsic G2B cues (\S\ref{sec:dead}).

\noindent\textbf{Internal validity.}
Dev AUROC of the LoRA model was saturated, so checkpoint selection fell back to dev loss and was weak.
Thresholds tuned on dev are descriptive: a secondary threshold shows how much a detector depends on calibration, not a deployable setting.
Single runs carry noise even at temperature 0: Qwen gives the same verdict in all three runs on \res{x}{rep_Qwen_L1}--\res{x}{rep_Qwen_L2} of the repeat units, so differences of a few units are not meaningful (Table~\ref{tab:repeat}).
CodeQL's 50-unit batching shared unrenamed Juliet helpers across units and produced 4 extra false alarms (\S\ref{sec:errors}).
LLM services can change behind a model name; we record model identifiers, prompt hashes and dates for every run.

\noindent\textbf{External validity and contamination.}
Juliet has been public for more than a decade~\cite{boland2012juliet} and is likely in the training data of every LLM we test.
Renaming identifiers, removing comments and the \Dead{} rewrite reduce verbatim recall, but a model may still recognize the templates.
That DeepSeek and Jev score higher on \Deb{} than on \Orig{} (Table~\ref{tab:orig}) suggests that recalled labels do not drive the results.
Our models are one API model, one 4B open model and two decision models.

\noindent\textbf{Coverage.}
Because of the \yen10 budget, DeepSeek is evaluated on \Dead, \Plac{} and \Orig{} only on \code{nested25} (\res{x}{n_nested25} units) in the paired comparisons, not on all 2,656 eval units.
\code{nested25} is stratified by flow category and mechanism, but its confidence intervals are wider.

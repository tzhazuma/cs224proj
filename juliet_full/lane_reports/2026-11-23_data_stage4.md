**Implemented.**
- **Predictions:** `results/<method>/<layer>/full_v1.jsonl` plus `.meta.json` for asan_lsan on layer1, stress, placebo and original; asan_lsan_default, valgrind and dynamic_enhanced on layer1; dynamic_enhanced on stress and placebo. - **Latency:** `lat300_c1` for asan_lsan, valgrind and dynamic_enhanced (300 units each), run at concurrency 1.
- **The bug:** `wcsdup` allocates inside glibc, so the old attribution took `wcsmbs/wcsdup.c` as the leak site. All 48 `strdup_wchar_t` bad units were missed by asan_lsan and dynamic_enhanced. `leak_site()` now skips frames with relative (glibc) paths; stdio buffers still go to `io.c`, which is outside the unit. I checked this on four units (`strdup_wchar_t_01` and `char_malloc_68`, bad and good, with `use_globals` on and off).
- **The rerun:** I removed the affected rows (backup at `/tmp/rows_full_v1_before_rerun.jsonl`) and reran the 336 `strdup_wchar_t` jobs across all layers. No other job had misattributed leak blocks. Both passes are recorded in the meta files.
- **Your flock note:** I killed the old latency waiter (it had measured nothing) and restarted it with `dynamic/latency.sh`. The quiet check now happens after the lock is held. The meta records lock wait (138.8 s), quiet wait (60 s) and load before/after (1.65 → 2.43). I had restarted it once more after the bulk run so it would use the fixed code.

**How the citation check works:** each superscript, footer entry and footer height is written to `build/main.sref`. `tools/check_citations.py` runs inside `make check` and fails if:
- a page's superscripts and footer entries don't match;
- one number points to two sources;
- numbers aren't 1..k in order of first mention;
- a footer is taller than two lines (19 pt);
- any section still uses the old macro.

An unknown source key stops the build.
